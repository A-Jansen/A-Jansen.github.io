ecoAIware

This website is part of a research project conducted by Anniek Jansen, Supraja Sankaran and Emiel Krahmer. The visualizations are based on data collected using a set of data physicalizations, ecoAIware, at an academic conference and a library.

Explore the data by hovering over the data points and see what each participant answered at the different locations. The correct answers, if applicable, are given in bold.

The tokens came in two colour shades: lighter colour shades indicate infrequent generative AI users (less than once a week) and darker colours for frequent (at least once a week) users. Outlined shapes indicate that the participant was featured with a quote in the pictorial.
